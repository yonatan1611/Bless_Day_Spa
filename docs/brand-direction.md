# Bless Day Spa — proposed visual direction

Grounded in the research record, the site should express an established local day spa in Wollo Sefer: quiet, practical, warm, and considered. It should not pose as a resort, clinic, or newly invented luxury brand.

## Design thesis

**A composed urban retreat, shaped by warmth and ritual.**

The visual system will use mineral neutrals, softened cocoa, muted olive, and a single deep ink tone. Editorial serif display type will be paired with a straightforward sans-serif for utility. The composition should leave generous breathing room, use honest surfaces, and keep calls to action visibly useful.

## Art direction

- **Photography:** a small number of full-bleed, documentary-style interiors or treatment details. Only rights-cleared Bless Day Spa photography may be used in production. Until supplied, use an explicit, non-public placeholder state rather than stock imagery.
- **Texture:** subtle paper/grain through CSS only, never decorative gradients.
- **Structure:** one strong hero, an asymmetric service index, a compact location panel, and a persistent mobile booking action.
- **Local specificity:** Wollo Sefer and Addis Ababa are named plainly where verified; no generic “escape” or destination-spa tropes.
- **Accessibility:** strong text contrast, keyboard-visible focus, reduced-motion support, 44px minimum touch targets, and semantic landmarks.

## Proposed content architecture

1. Home — factual introduction, service categories, a restrained visual field, directions, appointment request.
2. Services — only confirmed categories, with details held until the business approves a treatment menu.
3. Visit — Wollo Sefer / Ethio-China Road directional copy and an external Maps search.
4. Appointment request — lightweight form with preferred contact/time and clear non-confirmation state.

## Deliberate exclusions

No invented founder story, wellness outcomes, review carousel, awards, price list, facility claims, or social proof counters. No brand mark will be fabricated: the initial build should be wordmark-only until official artwork is supplied.
